# Five connected complexity layers

| Layer | Scientific role | Dependence | Why it is fair |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact disguised matrix pairs | Constant integer shears and legal scaling obscure a known canonical structure | All later stages use the same A/B pair | 18-by-18 bound, pinned symbolic tools, no precision threshold |
| Primary and singular module structure | Repeated irreducible powers, infinity partitions, repeated/zero indices and Hilbert growth | Determines the block pair and module dimensions | Explicit companion, reversal and transpose conventions |
| Constructive strict equivalence | Solve matrix-pair isomorphism constraints over Q and find invertible frames | Certifies the entire classification and constructs kernels | Any invertible rational frames accepted; prototype exact checks passed |
| Polynomial splitting | Saturated bases, duals and a reflexive inverse with polynomial projectors | Binds free variables and compatibility to one consistent solution map | Mathematical identities rather than an oracle normalization |
| Prime-power response obstructions | Distinguish no solution, polynomial solutions and necessary rational poles | Uses both kernel modules and the finite torsion denominator | Constructive upper bounds plus exact local lower bounds |

Large scaling is an exact-arithmetic trap with a research purpose, not a requirement for arbitrary floating-point digits. Small dimension and measured oracle timing leave a substantial margin below process limits. No unrelated physical simulation, file-hunting puzzle or networking failure is counted as mathematical difficulty.
