# Thirteen mathematical obligations

| Stage | Obligation | Mathematical inputs | Observable output | Scientific purpose | Plausible error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal rank | A,B | r and a nonsingular integer-point minor | Exact lower bound, sets defect dimensions | Specializing at a root or using numerical rank |
| 2 | Finite Smith invariants | P,r | monic d1|...|dr including units | Finite module torsion and global denominator | Wrong signs/units, repeated multiplicities |
| 3 | Finite primary divisors | Smith factors | monic irreducible p and powers | Build companion primary blocks; prime-power poles | Splitting p^e into e copies of p |
| 4 | Infinity partition | reversed pencil, rank | positive sizes and Weyr profile | Polynomial inverse degree at infinity | Treating rank B as the partition |
| 5 | Right filtration | P,r | right indices and degree-kernel Hilbert profile | Free polynomial solution generators | Ignoring shifts or zero/repeated indices |
| 6 | Left filtration | P transpose,r | left indices and Hilbert profile | Compatibility obstruction dimensions | Swapping left and right in rectangles |
| 7 | Constant strict equivalence | stages 1–6 | invertible U,V and exact pair identities | Construct an actual decomposition, not only invariants | A polynomial equivalence is not a constant frame |
| 8 | Saturated minimal modules | classification and a constructive approach | both bases, leading rank, polynomial duals | Complete kernels without spurious finite roots | Independent rational vectors can be nonsaturated |
| 9 | Reflexive resolvent | finite denominator and full decomposition | N/D, two inverse identities, polynomial PG/GP | Maps compatible forcings to solutions without projector poles | Arbitrary pseudoinverse introduces removable poles |
| 10 | Projector factorization | chosen bases, duals and resolvent | KR,KL with four cross identities | Connect complements to both kernel modules | Individually correct but mutually inconsistent outputs |
| 11 | Rational compatibility | left saturated module and f | classification or nonzero left obstruction | Determine whether a forced solution exists | Confusing polynomial and rational solvability |
| 12 | Minimum-denominator solution | compatibility, D, polynomial projectors | monic delta|D and Py=delta f | Attain an upper bound on every finite pole | Using a nonminimal common denominator |
| 13 | Local lower bounds | finite primary factors and chosen solution | one prime-power row per pole | Prove every pole unavoidable under ALL kernel corrections | A particular solution alone proves no optimality |

These are certificate obligations, not thirteen required commands or thirteen artificially serialized algorithms. Independent branches may be computed in any order. Stages 7–13 carry the main additional mathematical work. Every output is checked; none earns partial reward. The precise definitions and empty-dimensional conventions are in the public specification.
