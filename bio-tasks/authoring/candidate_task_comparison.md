# Six-candidate scientific selection

Two distinct creators developed their candidates concurrently after the coordinator physically inspected the source matrices and reconciled all120 GSM titles. The brief is a supplied project specification; it grants no authority to publish or use provider credentials. Exactly two task packages were selected.

| Creator / candidate | Objective and scientific value | Feasibility and likely QC risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1: perturbation specificity certificates | Test whether a construct-associated response clears parental negative-control equivalence and minimum-effect gates; bound early interactions under missing-control assumptions. | Four-cell contrasts at4/8/16/24 identifiable as relative-count associations; UT2 absent, requiring explicit sensitivity sets. Welch small-n assumptions, missing length offsets and all-negative certificates need transparent limits. Five coherent stages, seconds-scale reference, numerical verification feasible. | Selected; joint intersection-union probabilities and BY correction prevent false interpretation of separately selected lists. |
| A2: stable normalization anchors | Discover a robust normalization reference across stressors. | Biological importance high, but anchor selection can become circular and true stable abundance cannot be identified without external controls. Alternative correct answers difficult to grade objectively. | Rejected for weak ground truth. |
| A3: shared stress-response modules | Separate shared ISR-associated and stressor-specific time programs. | Computationally feasible but substantially overlaps the original publication's central time/dose modules. Mechanistic attribution unsupported by this series alone. | Rejected for conceptual duplication risk. |
| B1: cross-representation provenance | Recover count–TPM sample identities and diagnose sparse technical mixtures with calibration/discovery/validation gene separation. | Real paired representations establish identity; synthetic aliquots have planted provenance, explicitly labeled simulation. Effective-length mismatch demands an empirical pilot and held-out validation. Public source does not imply source mislabeling. | Selected after120/120 held-out identity recovery in pilot. |
| B2: effective-length sensitivity atlas | Describe gene/sample count–TPM log-ratio residuals and floor artifacts. | Descriptive residuals identifiable; actual isoform switching is not. Without transcript lengths or independent truth the discrete grading objective is weak. | Rejected as stand-alone task; limitations inform B1. |
| B3: prospective provenance gene panel | Choose a compact sensor panel robust across stress backgrounds. | Scientifically useful but panel optimization risks dominating biology; held-out scenarios and valid panel equivalence require substantial additional design. | Rejected in favor of B1's stronger ground truth. |

Selected objectives differ: A asks what statistical evidence supports an association-specificity claim under missing controls; B asks whether observed expression representations and unknown aliquots have recoverable provenance. A uses independent-group uncertainty, equivalence, joint-null testing, dependence-robust adjustment and partial identification. B uses robust nuisance calibration, global assignment, compositional sparse reconstruction and held-out residual validation. A outputs per-gene inference and sensitivity tables; B outputs sample mappings, mixture supports/weights, and residual/ambiguity certificates. A is checked against independent arithmetic and controlled inference errors; B against planted technical truth and independent reconstruction. Neither assigns one matrix to a nominally independent biological experiment.

The reference methods are established; originality is in the scientific task objective and integrated evaluation design, not in inventing new statistical formulas. Difficulty is intended expert-level and remains uncalibrated until authorized trials. The full feasibility, layer dependency, negative controls and resource evidence belong to each task's authoring records. The coordinator approved A1 with relative-count/effective-length and joint-FDR safeguards and B1 conditional on the real-data identity pilot; no threshold was tuned to force a biological discovery.
